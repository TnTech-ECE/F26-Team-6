## **Introduction**

## **Team Expectations**

In this section, the team will discuss and clearly define their collective expectations in various key areas. Be specific to ensure mutual understanding.

### **1. Participation** *(Engagement and Effort in the Project)*

- **Expectation:** Each team member is expected to:
  - For meetings, each member is expected to actively participate in group activities and discussions.
  - Notify the team before missing a meeting or obligation.
  - Take the lead in discussions regarding assigned portions of the project.

### **2. Contribution** *(Progress on the Project)*

- **Expectation:** Each team member is expected to:
  - Each member is expected to diligently complete tasks on time.
  - Share expertise and assist others when needed.
  - Contribute to the project as equitably as possible.

### **3. Communication**

- **Expectation:** Team communication should be:
  - For contact outside of school and formal meetings, the team will utilize a designated Discord server titled “ECE-Capstone” with supported chats to track activity of members and assignments done.
  - Within this server, all communication of each person is professional, respectful, and considerate.

### **4. Meetings**

- **Expectation:** Regarding team meetings:
  - The team will hold one in-person mandatory meeting per week with the time and day be during the scheduled capstone day we do not meet with our instructors.
  - Additional meetings may be conducted in-person or online to ensure work is being done and to provide updates to other members.

### **5. Conduct**

- **Expectation:** Team members will:
  - Treat each other with respect and courtesy.
  - Listen actively and allow others to express their ideas without interruption.
  - Address disagreements professionally and constructively.

### **6. Conflict Resolution**

- **Expectation:** The team will:
  - Address conflicts promptly through open dialogue.
  - Use a step-by-step approach to resolve issues:
    1. Discuss the issue privately between involved parties.
    2. If unresolved, bring the issue to the team for discussion.
    3. Seek input from the instructor if necessary.
  - Focus on finding mutually acceptable solutions.

### **7. Deadlines**

- **Expectation:** Regarding deadlines:
  - All team-set and instructor-set deadlines are to be met.
  - If a task is going to be late, the member responsible should notify team as courtesy and for help.
  - To ensure the following deadlines, the team will adjust the workload of members accordingly.
