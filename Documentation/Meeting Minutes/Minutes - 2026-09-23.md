# Meeting Minutes - September 23, 2026

## Meeting
Virtual Meeting with Valinor

## Participants
- Team 06
- Valinor representatives

## Discussion
The team held a virtual meeting with Valinor to discuss the project requirements and clarify the direction of the drone fault detection system.

Valinor confirmed that they are ready to sponsor the project and will allow the team to order necessary parts once the design is fully developed.

The team discussed the NVIDIA Jetson Nano as the processing platform for the machine learning system. The team confirmed that the Jetson Nano will be used as the main processing platform for the project.

Valinor clarified that the fault detection system does not need to operate in real time. Processing can take a few minutes if necessary, although faster results are preferred.

The physical dimensions of the drone dock were also provided:
- 30 inches wide
- 40 inches long
- 12–16 inches high

These dimensions will be considered when designing the camera placement, lighting, and other hardware for the system.

## Decisions
- The NVIDIA Jetson Nano will be used as the main processing platform.
- Valinor will sponsor necessary parts once the design is sufficiently developed.
- The fault detection system does not have to operate in real time.
- The team will design the camera and hardware setup around the dimensions of the drone dock.
- Team 06 and Valinor will hold bi-weekly check-in meetings to discuss progress and project requirements.

## Next Steps
- Continue developing the overall system design around the Jetson Nano.
- Research compatible cameras and other required hardware.
- Determine possible camera and lighting placement around the drone dock.
- Identify the parts that will need to be ordered through Valinor.
- Prepare for the next bi-weekly meeting with Valinor.
