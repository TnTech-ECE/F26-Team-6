# Meeting Minutes - October 28, 2026

## Meeting
Valinor Client Meeting

## Participants
- Team 6
- Valinor representatives

## Discussion
The team met with Valinor to clarify the hardware, software, and inspection requirements for the drone docking system. The client confirmed that the dock uses a Jetson Orin NX 16 GB board located on the backplate where the platform retracts. The board currently has unused computing capacity, so integrating the team's inspection software with the existing docking software may be possible. The team also discussed how the docking system detects the drone by checking electrical current when the drone is expected to be docked.

Valinor indicated that design files for the docking system are available and could be sent following the meeting. The team asked about access to the physical dock, possible modifications, power availability, external device connections, and wiring, but these details still need clarification.

The team discussed software privacy and licensing. Drone specifications are subject to a nondisclosure agreement, while any open-source code should remain generic and avoid disclosing protected information. Valinor expressed a preference for open-source software, with YOLOv4 identified as a possible starting point.

The client does not currently have a labeled dataset for PASS, WARN, and FAIL inspection results. The team discussed the possibility of obtaining a drone for testing and looking at the Skydio X10 and Teal Black Widow as reference platforms. The team also established a target minimum visible feature size of approximately 0.5 mm and an AI/ML inspection time of approximately 2–3 minutes. The specific defects that lighting should reveal will be determined by the team.

## Decisions
- The design will account for the Jetson Orin NX 16 GB already installed in the dock.
- The team will investigate whether its inspection software can share the existing board's computing resources.
- Open-source tools are preferred, with YOLOv4 considered as an initial option rather than a final selection.
- Any publicly released code must be generic and must not expose NDA-protected drone specifications.
- The team will plan around an approximately 0.5 mm minimum feature size and a 2–3 minute inspection target.
- The team will identify the defects that the lighting and inspection system should detect.
- The team will follow up on dock design files, hardware access, power and wiring constraints, modification limits, budget, and prototype timing.
- The team will investigate data collection options because no labeled inspection dataset is currently available.
